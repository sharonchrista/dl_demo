RGB CNN visualization: tiny cat image

This tab uses a small 8×8 RGB teaching image shaped like a cat face. Each pixel has three values: R, G, and B. A single RGB convolution filter therefore has depth 3: 3×3×3 = 27 multiplications for one output position.

1 · RGB cat image

Click a pixel to inspect its exact RGB values.

6 · 2×2 max pooling

The 6×6 activation map becomes 3×3 after non-overlapping 2×2 max pooling.

What students should notice

For a grayscale image, one 3×3 kernel uses 9 multiplications.

For RGB, each spatial kernel position has one weight for R, one for G, and one for B:

3 × 3 × 3 = 27 multiplications

The three channel contributions are summed to produce one feature-map value.
